# Financial-analystics-dashboard.
Project Overview
Developed an interactive Sales & Profit Performance Analysis Dashboard to analyze business performance, sales trends, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional performance.
-< a href="https://github.com/saravanansaravana2082-maker/Financial-analystics-dashboard./blob/main/Financial_Analytics_Dataset_2000_Rows.xlsx_Project.xlsx">Dataset</a>
Key Analysis

Analyzed ₹11.48M total sales and ₹2.30M total profit across 2,000 orders.
Compared sales and costs using total and average KPIs.
Analyzed monthly sales trends to identify high- and low-performing periods.
Compared sales performance across different product categories.
Evaluated profit contribution by region.
Analyzed order distribution across Customer Segments.
Visualized average profit margin by salesperson.
Added interactive filters for Region, Product Category, Order Date, Payment Method, and Salesperson.
